### Slide 03: What We Heard

* **Headline**: 14 / 16 interviewees had encountered suspicious online activity.
* **Context**: Qualitative customer discovery sample, Aug 16–26, 2026 *(Note: Qualitative interview findings represent reported user experiences from customer discovery and are not general population statistics.)*
* **Key Qualitative Findings**:
  * **Most Repeated Difficulty**: *"Scams can look very convincing."* Users noted that fraudulent messages frequently mimic official branding and tone.
  * **Trust & Verification Gap**: *"I don't know where to verify."* Users reported feeling uncertain about how or where to verify whether a website, offer, or business was genuine.
  * **Technical Barrier**: Cybersecurity terminology and indicators are intimidating and unclear to everyday users.
  * **Real Consequence**: Costly realization. Multiple interviewees reported losing funds to "make more money" schemes before realizing the fraud.
* **Source**: 16 customer discovery interviews, Aug 2026

### Slide 04: The Insight

* **Headline**: The problem isn’t just detecting scams. It’s knowing what to do before it’s too late.
* **Core Framework**:
  * **01 DETECT**: Identify requested sensitive actions and warning signs within message content.
  * **02 EXPLAIN**: Translate technical risks into plain, actionable language.
  * **03 PROTECT**: Restrict high-risk interactions within the Alert Lens application to prevent credential loss.
  * **04 VERIFY**: Provide direct paths to independent verification outside the message.
  * **05 LEARN**: Recognize identified patterns to avoid future risks.
* **Slide Number**: 04

### Slide 06: From Suspicious Message to Safe Decision

* **Simulated High-Risk Example**:
  > *"Congratulations! You have been selected for a ₦150,000 student bursary. Click the link below, log into your student account, enter your BVN and OTP to claim your payment."*
* **Detections**: Open link, Log into account, Provide BVN, Enter OTP
* **Workflow Steps**:
  * **Analysis**: **Risk: High**. Identified four sensitive requested actions: opening a link, logging into an account, providing BVN, and entering an OTP. The combination of a financial incentive and sensitive credential/identity requests increases the protection risk.
  * **Explanation**: *"Identity & Credential Harvest"* — The message requests secure login information and national identity information in connection with a financial offer.
  * **Protection (Block)**: The high-risk interaction path is restricted within the Alert Lens application, with evidence and safer verification guidance provided instead of direct navigation.
  * **Verification**: Official channel guidance directed to the official university portal or verified contact channel for independent confirmation.
  * **Golden Safety Rule**: *Never verify a suspicious message using links, phone numbers, or contact details provided inside the message itself.*
* **Slide Number**: 06

---

### Slide 07: Protection Beyond a Warning

* **Action Tiers**:
  * 🟢 **ALLOW (Safe Path)**: No major warning signs identified in the message or destination.
  * 🟡 **WARN (Caution)**: Unusual patterns identified. Insufficient evidence for a block, but requires user vigilance.
  * 🔴 **BLOCK (Restricted)**: High-risk interaction restricted within the application to prevent sensitive data exposure.
* **Engine Features**:
  * **URL Intelligence**: Examines observable URL characteristics including domain structure, IP-host detection, HTTP usage, Punycode/unusual-character detection, contextual domain-extension signals, tracking/referral indicators, and conservative brand-mismatch analysis.
  * **Procedure Detection**: Identifies what the message is asking for (BVN, OTP, Login) and evaluates the risk level. *Sensitive-action detection does not automatically mean fraud; context determines the protection decision.*
* **Important Disclaimer**: Detection and analysis represent prototype decisions, not an automatic fraud verdict.
* **Slide Number**: 07
